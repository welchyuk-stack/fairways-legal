Privacy Policy

Fairways: Golf Club Manager · last updated 17 October 2026

Fairways does not collect, store, share or receive any personal information about you. It does not require an account, and it has no servers of its own. The developer receives nothing from the app.

Your saved clubs and settings

Your settings (such as sound levels and graphics quality) are saved on your device. Your saved clubs are saved on your device too, and, if you're signed in to iCloud on your iPad, in Fairways' own folder in your iCloud account, so they follow you to another iPad and survive a reinstall. That copy is held by Apple in your iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy; the developer can't see or reach it. You can turn it off for Fairways in iOS Settings › [your name] › iCloud. None of this is ever sent to the developer or anyone else.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Fairways has no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics code.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send the developer anonymous crash reports. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off at any time in that setting.

Children

Fairways does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the developer at welchyuk@gmail.com.
