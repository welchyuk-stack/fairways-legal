Common questions

Where are my saved clubs kept?
On your iPad and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in Fairways' folder in your own iCloud account, so they come back after a reinstall or on another iPad. Without iCloud they live only on the iPad, and deleting the app deletes them.

Golfers are walking off.
They gave up waiting. Check the tee sheet and pace of play in the Course window: more holes, a marshal, or wider tee-time spacing all help.

Why are fewer golfers coming?
Visitors react to tangible things: the number of holes, grass condition, the green fee against what each type of golfer thinks is fair, facilities and waiting times. The Handbook inside the game explains each one.

The game feels warm or the battery drains quickly.
Lower the graphics quality in Settings, or turn on Low Power Mode, which caps it automatically.

Does it need the internet?
No. Fairways plays fully offline; iCloud only syncs your saved clubs when it can. See the privacy policy.

What does "Share anonymous stats" send?
Only if you said Share: what's played, for how long, and crash reports, with nothing about you and no names. Turn it off any time in Settings › Game; the privacy policy has the full list.
